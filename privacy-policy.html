Penguin Drift — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 Developer: Hüseyin Talha Avşar Contact: avsarhuseyintalha@gmail.com

Short answer

Penguin Drift collects no data. No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no internet connection. The game runs entirely on your device.

Long answer

Personal data collected

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any of the following:

  • Name, email, phone number or any other identifier
  • Location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
  • Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA)
  • Usage statistics, crash logs, analytics

The only things stored on your device

Four small files are kept inside the app's own folder on your device:

  • game progress — which levels you have finished and your best move count,
  • lives — how many lives you have and when the next one refills,
  • wardrobe — the penguin color, hat and neckwear you chose,
  • unfinished levels — the board and move count of a level you left, so you

continue where you stopped.

Lives are refilled using your device's own clock; the app does not ask any server for the time. These files:

  • never leave the device through the app; the app sends them to no

server. Like all app data, they are included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on,

  • cannot be read by anyone, including the developer,
  • are deleted along with the app when you remove it.

They are game-state files, not personal data; they contain nothing that identifies you.

Internet access

The app makes no network requests. There is no server. It behaves identically in airplane mode.

Advertising and tracking

No ads are shown and no tracking takes place. The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask for.

Third-party services

None. The app depends on no third-party SDKs: no ad network, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Children

The app is suitable for ages 4 and up. Because no data is collected, no children's data is collected either. This satisfies COPPA and the children's provisions of the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK by design.

Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Since no personal data is processed, there is no data subject to a deletion, correction or portability request. If you wish to remove these files from your device, deleting the app is sufficient.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's behaviour changes. In particular, if rewarded advertising is added in future, this document will be rewritten before that release — an ad network brings its own data collection, and several of the statements above would no longer hold.
